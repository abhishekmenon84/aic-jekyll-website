    <!-- Overview -->
    <div class="mb-16 reveal">
      <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mb-4" style="font-family:'Playfair Display',serif; color:var(--charcoal);">Project Overview</h2>
      <p class="text-stone-600 leading-relaxed mb-4">
        <strong>STAND STRONG</strong> is a youth-led anti-racism education and community engagement initiative designed and delivered by the AIC Youth Leadership Group in Fredericton. The project gives young people practical, real-world tools to recognize racism and discrimination, respond safely and calmly, support people who experience racism, and become positive bystanders and community leaders.
      </p>
      <p class="text-stone-600 leading-relaxed mb-4">
        The program combines interactive workshops, scenario-based learning, peer education, storytelling, community discussions, and digital resources. The emphasis is on <strong>practical skills rather than awareness alone</strong>: participants practice what they can actually do when they witness or experience racism in schools, workplaces, online spaces, or community settings.
      </p>
      <p class="text-stone-600 leading-relaxed">
        The project is intentionally youth-led. Young leaders make key project decisions, develop workshop content, facilitate activities, and build educational materials, with organizational support and mentorship from the Indo-Canadian Association of Fredericton (AIC).
      </p>
    </div>
